NOW IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. Appointment & Authority:
   - The Seller appoints the Company as their authorized platform/agent to market, verify, and 
     facilitate the sale of the Property under RERA guidelines.

2. Title Inspection & Cooperation:
   - The Seller agrees to furnish all original title deeds, revenue extracts (7/12, Khatauni, Jamabandi), 
     encumbrance certificates, and partition deeds to the Company's legal team for verification.

3. Brokerage Fee & GST:
   - The Seller agrees to pay the Company a service/brokerage fee of 1% (One Percent) of the total 
     agreed sale price plus 18% GST.
   - The fee shall become due and payable upon the signing of the Agreement to Sell or execution of 
     the registered Sale Deed, whichever is agreed upon in writing.

4. Indemnity by Seller:
   - The Seller warrants that the Property is free from undisclosed mortgages, family litigation, 
     boundary disputes, or government acquisition notices.
