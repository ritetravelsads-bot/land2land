CHANNEL PARTNER AGREEMENT
(Master Channel Partner Agreement - Associate Broker)

THIS AGREEMENT is made on this _____ day of _______, 20, by and between:
[YOUR COMPANY NAME] PVT. LTD., having its registered office at ________________________
(hereinafter referred to as the "Company"),

AND

MR./MS./M/S. ________________________, residing/having office at ________________________, PAN: 
____________, GSTIN (if applicable): ____________ (hereinafter referred to as the "Associate Broker").

WHEREAS:

A. The Company operates a tech platform aggregating clean, title-verified land transactions and guarantees 
   dispute-free land titles to buyers.

B. The Associate Broker agrees to act as a field associate to source land listings, facilitate site visits, and 
   coordinate with property owners/buyers.

NOW IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. Scope of Work:
   - The Associate Broker shall upload genuine land details, survey/Khasra numbers, and owner 
     contact details onto the Company portal.
   - The Associate Broker shall coordinate physical site inspections for buyers referred by the 
     Company.

2. Commercial Terms & Commission Split:
   - The Company collects 1% from the Seller and 1% from the Buyer (+ 18% GST).
   - Upon successful execution and legal registration of the Sale Deed, the Company shall pay the 
     Associate Broker a Commission of 1% of the total land deal value.
   - Tax Deductions: The Company shall deduct 5% TDS under Section 194H of the Income Tax Act 
     before releasing the net payment to the Associate Broker. If the Associate Broker is GST-registered, 
     payment will be made against a valid GST invoice.

3. Non-Circumvention & Lead Protection:
   - The Associate Broker shall not directly or indirectly deal with any Buyer or Seller introduced 
     through the Company platform outside of this Agreement.
   - Any attempt to bypass the Company shall result in immediate termination, forfeiture of pending 
     payouts, and legal recovery damages.

4. Representation & Verification:
   - The Associate Broker warrants that preliminary information provided about any land parcel is true 
     to the best of their knowledge. The final legal title verification and dispute guarantee shall be 
     conducted exclusively by the Company.
